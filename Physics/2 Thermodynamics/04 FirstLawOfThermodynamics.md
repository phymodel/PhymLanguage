# First Law of Thermodynamics

## Formula

$$
\boxed{
\Delta U = Q + W
}
$$

## First Law of Thermodynamics

- First law of thermodynamics: the increase in the internal energy of a thermodynamic system equals the sum of the heat transferred to it by the surroundings and the work done on it by the surroundings.
- $\Delta U$: the change in the internal energy of the system
- $Q$: the heat transferred from the surroundings to the system
- $W$: the work done on the system by the surroundings
