# Spherical Refraction

## Formula

$$
\boxed{
\frac{n_1}{u}+\frac{n_2}{v}=\frac{n_2-n_1}{R}
}
$$

## Spherical Refraction Formula (single spherical surface)

Expanding Snell's law under the "paraxial ray" approximation ($\theta$ is small, $\sin\theta\approx\theta$) for a single spherical interface gives:
$$\frac{n_1}{u}+\frac{n_2}{v}=\frac{n_2-n_1}{R}$$
This is the first simplification derived from Snell's law.
