# Free Fall Formulas

## Formula

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Free-fall velocity–time: } &v=gt \\
\text{Free-fall displacement–time: } &x=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2 \\
\text{Free-fall velocity–displacement: } &v^2=2gx
\end{aligned}
}
$$

## The Three Formulas of Free Fall

From the three kinematic formulas

$$
\begin{aligned}
& v = v_0 + at, \\
& x = v_0 t + \dfrac{1}{2} a t^2, \\
& v^2 - v_0^2 = 2 a x.
\end{aligned}
$$

with $v_0=0$ and $a=g$ we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
& v=gt, \\
& x=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2, \\
& v^2=2gx.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Example 1

(**2013 National Syllabus Paper, Q 2-19**) (Multiple choice) Two small balls $A$ and $B$ are thrown vertically upwards, one after the other, with the same speed, from points at different heights above the ground, the two throws being $2\,\rm s$ apart; the graphs of their motions are the straight lines $A$ and $B$ shown in the figure. Then ($\qquad$).

![example1](./07 example1.html)

A. At $t=2\rm\,s$ the difference in height of the two balls must be $40\,\rm m$
B. At $t=4\,\rm s$ the displacements of the two balls from their own points of throw are equal
C. The time intervals the two balls take from the throw to reaching the ground are equal
D. The time interval from the throw to the highest point is the same for ball $A$ and for ball $B$
**Analysis**
(1) At $t=2\,\rm s$ the displacement of ball $A$ from its point of throw is $x_\text{A}=\dfrac{1}{2}\times(30+10)\times 2\,\rm m=40\,\rm m$, and the displacement of ball $B$ from its point of throw is $x_\text{B}=0\,\rm m$; the difference of the displacements is 40 m, but neither point of throw is fixed, so the difference in height of the two balls is not necessarily 40 m. A is wrong.
(2) At $t=4\,\rm s$ the displacement of ball $A$ from its point of throw is $x_\text{A}=\dfrac{1}{2}\times30\times 3\,\rm m +\dfrac{1}{2}\times1\times (-10)\,\rm m=40\,\rm m$, and the displacement of ball $B$ from its point of throw is $x_\text{B}=\dfrac{1}{2}\times(30+10)\times 2\rm\,m=40\,\rm m$, so the displacements of the two balls from their own points of throw are equal. B is correct.
(3) By the formula $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$, the heights $h$ of the points of throw are different, so the time intervals $t$ are different. C is correct.
(4) The graph shows that the time interval from the throw to the highest point is the same for ball $A$ and for ball $B$, namely $3\,\rm s$ in both cases. D is correct.
**Answer** BD.
**Formula** free fall $x=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$.
