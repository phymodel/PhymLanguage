# Acceleration Definition

## Formula

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Change of velocity: } &\Delta v = v_2 - v_1 \\
\text{Acceleration: } &a=\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}
\end{aligned}
}
$$

## Change of Velocity

- Formula for the change of velocity: $\Delta v = v_2 - v_1$.

## Acceleration

- Definition formula: $a=\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$.
- Definition: the ratio of the change of velocity $\Delta v$ to the time $\Delta t$ taken by that change is called the acceleration $a$ (the slope).
- Physical meaning: the physical quantity that describes how fast the velocity of a body changes.

## Example 1

**(AI-assisted, 2026.10.20)** The speed of a car decreases from $20\,\text{m/s}$ to $8\,\text{m/s}$ in $4\,\text{s}$. Find its acceleration.

**Given:** $\Delta t=4\,\text{s}$, $v_1=20\,\text{m/s}$, $v_2=8\,\text{m/s}$.
**To find:** the acceleration $a$.
**Solution:**
$$
a=\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{8\,{\rm m/s}-20\,{\rm m/s}}{4\,{\rm s}}=-3\,{\rm m/s}^2.
$$
The minus sign means that the acceleration is opposite to the velocity.
**Answer:** the acceleration is $-3\,\text{m/s}^2$, and the car is decelerating.
