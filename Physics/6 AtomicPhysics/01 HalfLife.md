# Half-Life

## Formula

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{$\alpha$ decay: } &{^A_Z X\to^{A-4}_{Z-2}Y+^4_2He} \\
\text{$\beta$ decay: } &{^A_ZX\to^{\,\,\,\,\,A}_{Z+1}Y+^{\,\,\,0}_{-1}e} \\
\text{$\gamma$ decay: } &{^1_0\rm{n}\to\, ^1_1H\,+\,^{\,\,\,0}_{-1}e} \\
\text{Half-life: } &N=N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{\tau}}
\end{aligned}
}
$$

Note: the mass number $A$ and the charge number $Z$ are conserved.
